Our Story

We Don't Just Book Trips — We Create Memories

Founded in 2025 in Ghaziabad, Travel On Wings began with a simple but powerful belief: every journey should be as remarkable as the destination. What started as a small travel consultancy has grown into one of India's most trusted travel companies, serving 100 happ travellvers.

Our founders, passionate travellers themselves, were frustrated by the cookie-cutter approach of most agencies. They set out to build something different — a company where every single trip is treated as unique, every traveller's preferences are heard, and every detail is handled with care and expertise.

Today, with a team of 40+ travel specialists and partnerships with leading hotels, airlines and tour operators worldwide, we continue to deliver exceptional travel experiences to families, couples, solo adventurers and corporate clients alike.

Our Mission

To make exceptional travel accessible to every Indian, with transparent pricing and personalised service.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted and loved travel company, known for creating transformative journey experiences.
