Thailand

Ancient temples, tropical islands & the Land of Smiles beckons

The Land of Smiles

Thailand is one of the world's most diverse travel destinations, offering everything from glittering golden temples in Bangkok and lush elephant sanctuaries in Chiang Mai, to postcard-perfect turquoise islands in Phuket and Koh Samui. This cultural and natural richness, combined with legendary Thai warmth and hospitality, makes it perfect for every type of traveller.

Bangkok — one of the world's most dynamic cities — is a sensory overload of ornate royal temples, bustling street food markets, vibrant nightlife and world-class shopping malls. A short flight or train ride takes you to the serene north, where jungle treks, hill tribe villages and ethical elephant sanctuaries await.

Must-Experience

Thailand Highlights

Grand Palace & Temples

Explore Bangkok's breathtaking Grand Palace complex and the sacred Wat Phra Kaew (Emerald Buddha Temple), Thailand's most revered site.

Phi Phi Islands

Cruise to the iconic Phi Phi archipelago — limestone cliffs, emerald bays and the famous Maya Bay as seen in "The Beach."

Elephant Sanctuaries

Visit ethical elephant sanctuaries in Chiang Mai where rescued elephants roam freely — a responsible and deeply moving experience.

Street Food Culture

Sample Pad Thai, som tum, mango sticky rice and boat noodles at Bangkok's legendary night markets — a culinary journey like no other.

Floating Markets

Glide through Bangkok's colourful Damnoen Saduak and Amphawa floating markets — a centuries-old tradition still vibrant today.

Krabi & Railay Beach

Discover Krabi's dramatic limestone karst scenery — accessible only by boat, Railay Beach is one of Southeast Asia's most stunning spots.
