Maldives

Overwater bliss, coral reefs & the clearest waters in the world

Paradise on Earth

The Maldives — a nation of 1,192 coral islands scattered across the Indian Ocean — is widely regarded as one of the most beautiful places on Earth. It is famous for its impossibly blue lagoons, pristine white sandy beaches and rich marine biodiversity beneath the surface.

Staying in an overwater bungalow here is a bucket-list experience like no other. Step directly from your villa onto a glass-floor deck, watch fish glide beneath you, and fall asleep to the sound of gentle waves. Snorkelling and diving bring you face-to-face with manta rays, whale sharks and spectacular coral gardens.

Travel On Wings creates bespoke Maldives packages from India — from budget-friendly island resorts to ultra-luxury all-inclusive retreats. We handle flights, speedboat/seaplane transfers, accommodation and all activities, so your only job is to relax.

Maldives Highlights

Overwater Bungalows

Sleep suspended above the ocean in a glass-floored water villa — the iconic Maldivian experience that defines luxury travel.

Snorkelling & Diving

Explore vibrant coral reefs teeming with manta rays, sea turtles, reef sharks and hundreds of fish species in crystal-clear water.

Bio-luminescent Beach

Walk along Vaadhoo Island's famous "Sea of Stars" beach at night, where plankton illuminates the shoreline with a magical blue glow.

Whale Shark Encounters

Swim alongside the world's largest fish in the warm waters around Ari Atoll — a truly humbling natural encounter.

Sunset Dolphin Cruises

Set sail on a traditional dhoni at golden hour and watch spinner dolphins leap beside your boat as the sun sinks into the horizon.

Overwater Spa

Indulge in couples' spa treatments suspended over the lagoon — a uniquely Maldivian way to restore body and soul.
