Where Luxury Meets the Desert

Dubai is the jewel of the Middle East — a city that has transformed itself from a modest fishing village into one of the world's most visited destinations in just a few decades. It is home to the world's tallest building, the largest shopping mall, and some of the most extraordinary feats of modern architecture ever conceived.

Yet Dubai offers far more than its famous skyline. The surrounding Arabian desert is breathtakingly beautiful, offering camel rides, dune bashing, Bedouin-style dinners under the stars and sandboarding experiences that thrill every age. Meanwhile, traditional Old Dubai — with its gold souk, spice market and historic creek — offers a fascinating glimpse into Emirati culture.

Dubai Highlights

Burj Khalifa & Sky Views

Ascend to level 148 of the world's tallest building for panoramic views stretching from the desert to the Arabian Gulf.

Desert Safari Adventure

Experience dune bashing in a 4x4, camel riding, sandboarding, and a traditional Bedouin dinner under a billion stars.

World-Class Shopping

Explore Dubai Mall (the world's largest), Gold Souk, Spice Souk and boutique malls in Jumeirah — a shoppers' paradise.

Palm Jumeirah & Atlantis

Walk or drive the iconic palm-shaped island, visit Atlantis resort and enjoy its famous Aquaventure waterpark.

Dubai Creek Heritage Tour

Take an abra (traditional wooden boat) across the historic Creek to explore Al Bastakiya, the Gold Souk and Spice Market.

World's Best Dining

From Michelin-starred restaurants to authentic Emirati cuisine and rooftop bars — Dubai's food scene is extraordinary.
