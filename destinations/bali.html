Discover the Magic of Bali

Bali is one of Indonesia's 17,000 islands and one of the world's most beloved tropical destinations. Known as the "Island of the Gods," it enchants visitors with its dramatic landscapes — terraced rice paddies carved into hillsides, smoking volcanoes, dense jungles, long sandy beaches and warm turquoise water.

Beyond its natural beauty, Bali is a deeply spiritual place. Ancient Hindu temples dot every village and hilltop, and daily offerings fill the streets with the scent of incense and flower petals. Whether you're seeking adventure, romance, culture or pure relaxation, Bali delivers it all in abundance.

Must-Experience

Bali Highlights

Ancient Temples

Visit the iconic Tanah Lot sea temple at sunset, the cliffside Uluwatu temple and the sacred spring temple Tirta Empul.

Tegallalang Rice Terraces

Walk through UNESCO-recognised subak irrigation rice terraces carved into the Ubud hillside — a photographer's paradise.

World-Class Surfing

Kuta, Seminyak and Canggu offer legendary surf breaks suitable for beginners to pros, with professional coaching available.

Luxury Spa & Wellness

Bali is the spa capital of Asia. Enjoy traditional Balinese massages, flower baths and holistic healing at world-class resorts.

Sacred Monkey Forest

Wander through the mystical Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary in Ubud — home to over 700 Balinese long-tailed monkeys.

Balinese Cuisine

Savour babi guling (suckling pig), nasi campur and fresh seafood barbecues on Jimbaran beach as the sun sets over the Indian Ocean.
