Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tour package with Travel On Wings? +

Simply fill out the enquiry form on this page or call us directly. Our travel expert will get in touch within 2 hours to understand your requirements and create a personalised itinerary. After approval, you can confirm with a partial advance payment.

Do you arrange visa assistance? +

Yes! We provide comprehensive visa assistance for all major countries including USA, UK, Schengen, Australia, Thailand, Bali, Dubai and more. We guide you through the entire documentation process, schedule appointments and track your application.

Are your packages customisable? +

Absolutely. Every package we create is fully customisable. You can choose your hotels, activities, travel dates, add or remove days, and adjust the itinerary to suit your budget and preferences. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all travel.

What is the cancellation & refund policy? +

Cancellation policies vary depending on the destination and time of cancellation relative to departure. We always recommend purchasing travel insurance. Our team will share the specific cancellation terms at the time of booking. We strive to offer maximum flexibility.

Do you offer support during the trip? +

Yes! All our packages include 24/7 emergency travel support via WhatsApp and phone. For fully guided group tours, a dedicated tour manager accompanies you throughout. You're never alone when you travel with us.
